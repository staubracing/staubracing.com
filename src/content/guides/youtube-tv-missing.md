---
title: "YouTube TV is Missing"
description: "The YouTube TV app disappeared from your TV. Let's find it."
order: 2
icon: "📺"
---

1. **Pick up the Fire remote.** Find the black remote with the Amazon logo. It has a circle button in the middle.

2. **Press the Home button.** Look for the button that looks like a little house. Press it once.

3. **Scroll to Find.** Press the left button on the circle until you see a picture of a magnifying glass and the word "Find." Press the circle button.

4. **Search for YouTube TV.** A keyboard will appear on the screen. Type **Y-O-U-T-U-B-E** using the arrows and circle button. You'll see YouTube TV appear below.

5. **Select YouTube TV.** Press the down button until YouTube TV is highlighted. Then press the circle button.

6. **Open the app.** You'll see a button that says "Go to channel" or "Open." Press the circle button to start watching.
