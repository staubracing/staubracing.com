---
title: "Reset the Internet"
description: "TV says no internet? Let's fix it by restarting the modem."
order: 3
icon: "🔌"
---

1. **Find the modem.** Look for the small black box with blinking lights. It's usually near where the TV cable comes out of the wall.

2. **Unplug the power cord.** Find the black power cord coming out of the back of the modem. Pull it out of the modem or the wall outlet.

3. **Wait 30 seconds.** Count slowly to 30. This gives the modem time to fully shut down.

4. **Plug it back in.** Push the power cord back into the modem or wall outlet.

5. **Wait for the lights.** Watch the lights on the front of the modem. They will blink for about 2 minutes. When they stop blinking and stay on, the internet is working again.

6. **Try the TV again.** Go back to your TV and try watching something.
