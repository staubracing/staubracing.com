---
title: "Change TV Input"
description: "TV shows the wrong thing? You might need to switch inputs."
order: 4
icon: "📡"
---

1. **Find the TV remote.** This is the long remote that came with the TV, not the black Fire remote. It might say Samsung, LG, or Vizio on it.

2. **Press the Input button.** Look for a button that says "Input" or "Source." It might also have a picture of a box with an arrow. Press it once.

3. **Look at the TV screen.** A small menu will appear showing different options like HDMI 1, HDMI 2, or TV.

4. **Find the right input.** Try each one until you see the Fire TV home screen with all your apps. Usually it's **HDMI 1** or **HDMI 2**.

5. **Press OK or wait.** Some TVs need you to press OK. Others will switch automatically after a few seconds.

**Tip:** If you can't find the TV remote, look for an Input button on the bottom or side of the TV itself.
