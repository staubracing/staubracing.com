---
title: "Firestick Says 'Home is Currently Unavailable'"
description: "TV showing an error on the Firestick? Here's how to fix it by reconnecting to the internet."
order: 5
icon: "📺"
---

1. **Grab the TV remote.** Not the Firestick remote — the regular TV remote.

2. **Press the Input button.** Keep pressing it until you see the words **Fire TV Stick** on screen. That means you're on the right screen.

3. **Open the menu.** Look for the three lines (☰) in the upper left corner of the screen. Click on them.

4. **Go to Settings.** Scroll down in the menu until you see **Settings** and click it.

5. **Select Network.** Find and click on **Network**.

6. **Check your internet connection.** Look for **SpectrumSetup-B3** in the list. It should say **Connected** next to it.
   - If it does not say Connected, click on it to connect.

7. **Press the Home button.** Use the Firestick remote and press the **Home** button (the house icon).

8. **You're done!** Pictures, shows, and apps should all appear again. If not, try [resetting the modem](/guides/reset-modem).
