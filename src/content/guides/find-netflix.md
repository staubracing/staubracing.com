---
title: "Find Netflix"
description: "Can't find Netflix on the TV? Let's get you watching."
order: 1
icon: "🎬"
---

1. **Pick up the Fire remote.** Find the black remote with the Amazon logo. It has a circle button in the middle.

2. **Press the Home button.** Look for the button that looks like a little house. Press it once. The TV screen will show your apps.

3. **Find the Netflix app.** Look for the red square that says **Netflix**. It might be on the first row. If you don't see it, press the down button on the circle to look at more apps.

4. **Click on Netflix.** When you see the red Netflix square, press the circle button in the middle of the remote.

5. **Pick what to watch.** Use the up and down buttons to find something to watch. Press the circle button to start it.
